Getting Started with eulob

Introduction

eulob provides offline access to EU-LOB, a catalogue of names and entries from European lobbying and transparency registers.

The catalogue contains 19 territorial scopes and 21 registers. Its entries should not automatically be interpreted as unique legal organizations.

Load the package

library(eulob)
eulob_version()
#> [1] '0.2.1'

Filter the catalogue by country:

#> 1      IT              Camera dei Deputati     455        455

eulob_sources(country = "ES")
#>   country                              register n_names n_entities
#> 1      ES Spanish CNMC Interest Groups Register     622        621

eulob_sources() reports register-level counts using the existing catalogue indexes.

Additional approximate name-search methods include Jaccard, Jaro-Winkler and Damerau-Levenshtein.

These methods compare text, not semantic representations or verified legal identities.

Search normalized-name candidates

eulob_search_normalized() searches a separate bundled candidate index.

It supports three modes:

  • strict: strict normalized-name candidates.
  • exploratory: exploratory candidate matching.
  • both: both candidate categories.
head(eulob_search_normalized(
  "association",
  method = "strict",
  limit = 5
))
#>  [1] country               register              lobby_id             
#>  [4] name                  matched_name          match_type           
#>  [7] similarity            evidence              match_level          
#> [10] requires_match_review identity_verified     candidate_relevance  
#> [13] candidate_relation    candidate_filter     
#> <0 rows> (or 0-length row.names)

Candidate results are intended to assist manual assessment. They are not evidence of verified legal identity.

Understand identifiers

The catalogue distinguishes:

  • name_id: identifier for a normalized name.
  • name_normalized: normalized name representation.
  • name: recorded name.
  • country: territorial or supranational scope.
  • register: source register.
  • lobby_id: register-specific identifier.

Some register identifiers originate from official registration systems; others are derived from source records.

Identifiers should always be interpreted within their corresponding register.

Reproducibility

The bundled catalogue can be queried without an internet connection. Record the package version, search methods and filters used in any analysis.

eulob_info()
#> $project
#> [1] "EU-LOB"
#> 
#> $full_name
#> [1] "An Open and Incremental Database of Registered Lobbying Organizations in the European Union"
#> 
#> $package
#> [1] "eulob"
#> 
#> $version
#> [1] "0.2.1"
#> 
#> $repository
#> [1] NA
#> 
#> $data_version
#> [1] NA
eulob_version()
#> [1] '0.2.1'

For details on the indexing structure and methodological limitations, see Understanding the EU-LOB Catalogue.
